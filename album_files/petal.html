About ⋆ . ˚


Petal (stylized in all lowercase) is the eighth studio album by American singer-songwriter Ariana Grande. It was released on July 31, 2026, via her personal sub-label BabyDoll Music, under Republic Records.


A shift towards darker sounds, the album's lyrics were entirely written by Grande, with ILYA producing. The album explores themes of intrusive fan behavior, her portrayal in the press, and past relationships.


Following the release of Eternal Sunshine Deluxe: Brighter Days Ahead in 2025, Grande focused on promotions for Wicked: For Good, filming for Focker In-Law, recording lines for Oh, The Places You'll Go! and more R.E.M. Beauty cosmetics, and upon her return to the music, she never announced a new studio album after the release of the deluxe album. However, on January 1, 2026, fans who had signed up to her newsletter had received an email teasing new music for the upcoming year.


Background ⋆ . ˚


On February 28, 2026, Ariana Grande updated her Instagram bio for the first time in three years, to “see you this summer ꕤ｡˚⋆". The update appeared to tease the upcoming Eternal Sunshine Tour and suggested that the Wicked era may have come to an end. That same day, she unpinned posts related to Wicked and replaced them with footage from her Eternal Sunshine era.


Fans quickly noticed that the bio change happened on the 28th, sparking speculation that the number could hint at a forthcoming eighth studio album. Adding to the intrigue, Grande began commenting eight emojis under various videos and even on her Instagram story, a move she last made at the start of her Eternal Sunshine era. Celebrating two years of Eternal Sunshine, she mentioned that it was her "favorite project yet."


On March 8, 2026, Grande confirmed that she got new tattoos on her left palm, one of which is a flower with exactly eight petals. Later that day she commented "i have 8 glossy balms in my bag today" and "i realize what i've done by using that number but that is genuinely the count", acknowledging the theories but not disproving them.


On March 18, 2026, Grande posted a photo dump via her Instagram profile, exactly on 8 AM PST. The last time she did this was in 2023 to promote her then upcoming seventh studio album, posting photo dumps on every 7th, 17th and 27th. Right after, Max Martin reposted it on his Instagram profile, confirming it really was the exact studio where Grande recorded a couple songs for her fourth studio album Sweetener in 2018. Also, when you copy the flower emoji from Grande's Instagram biography and paste it, it shows the number 8 instead of the flower emoji.


On April 8, 2026, Grande posted 8 pictures of her in the studio at 8 AM PST. There were nine pictures in total, with the eighth photo being some kind of painted Chamomile flowers. Ilya Salmanzadeh has reposted the post right after it came out. On April 12, 2026, PopBase reported on X that Grande allegedly finished her album, which is set to be released this summer. The article that PopBase got this information from was TMZ. On April 18, 2026, Grande posted a video of her in the studio, along with a snippet of her recording a song, possibly the lead single for the album. In the video she says to Max Martin "What? What? Are you excited?" Then she is seen pitching her album to her label stating the theme of the album is "something that is full of life and growing through the cracks of something cold, and hard and challenging"


On April 20, 2026, Grande's team created an Instagram account, and their first post was a picture of flowers with the caption "@teamariana is officially on IG ꕤ stay tuned". They posted at exactly 8 PM EST, further continuing the 8 trend and for the promotion of the upcoming album. On April 21, 2026, Grande's team moved the Eternal Sunshine instrumental and a capella versions to the "Compilations" section of her Spotify page. On April 27, 2026, around 8 PM EST, Grande unpinned one of her posts from her Instagram feed, leaving only the Eternal Sunshine Deluxe: Brighter Days Ahead announcement and "Past Life" isolated vocals. Around the same time, the Brighter Days Hotline changed its audio from "We're counting down the 8s" to just 8 beeps.


On April 28, 2026, 8 AM PST, Grande officially announced her eighth studio album, titled petal in all lowercase, the album cover artwork, and a release date of July 31, 2026. The album was made available for pre-saving on streaming services, revealing that it will contain 12 tracks. Additionally, an album teaser video called "petal in the pavement" was uploaded to Apple Music. The album was also made available on her website, with a digital download, signed CD and vinyl editions, a cassette, standard CD, and two vinyl variants with different covers. The album debuted on the Spotify pre-save chart with 377.1K pre-saves, the second most for an album ever, since the chart launched earlier in the year. It ended its first week with just over 1 million pre-saves.


Later on May 5th, Grande unveiled the official Target exclusive album cover. She also unveiled exclusive Amazon, Urban Outfitters and Indie Exclusive Versions of the Album with the standard album cover. Three days later, on May 8, 2026, 8 AM PST, Grande announced the lead single "Hate That I Made You Love Me", with a release date of May 29, 2026. The song was made avaliable with an alternate cover and six physical versions for pre-order. On May 9, Ariana was seen in New York with makeup artist Daniel Chinchilla, who worked with her starting from My Everything. The next day, she was asked if the album was similar to Positions, to which she replied: "No, but I love her!.. I just enjoy evolving."


On May 18, 2026, Grande posted a video of the "Hate That I Made You Love Me" photos with some instrumental playing in the background at 8 AM PST. That same morning, Spotify sent out emails to Ariana's top fans to preorder the Spotify Fans First vinyl for the album, the first time she has ever had such a release. Also, the album track list preview was uploaded to TikTok. Additionally, the signed and webstore exclusive versions of the physical music variants were restocked.


On May 21, 2026, a sign-up link for "Petal surprises coming soon" was posted by Team Ariana. Two days later, on May 23, 2026, a teaser for the "Hate That I Made You Love Me" video was posted by Team Ariana. On May 26, 2026, Grande teased more lyrics from the single, and the song was uploaded to streaming services, confirming the length. Additionally, an animated cover, with a slight zoom on the album cover, was uploaded to Apple Music.


Promotion ⋆ . ˚


On May 29, 2026, the lead single, "Hate That I Made You Love Me" was released alongside a lyric video. The song debuted at #1 on US iTunes, and became her 23rd song to reach #1 on worldwide iTunes. It also became her 9th song to reach #1 on the global Spotify chart, with 8.78 million filtered streams, along with reaching #1 in the United States, with 2.8 million streams. Upon the release of the single, the album reached 2 million pre-saves on Spotify, becoming the 4th most pre-saved album of all time since the platform made that data public in September of 2025. The single debuted at #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, becoming Ariana's milestone 10th #1 hit, along with #1 on the Digital Song Sales chart, the Global 200, and Global 200 Excluding US charts.


Upon embarking on The Eternal Sunshine Tour on June 6, 2026, Ariana performed the lead single. She later begun teasing the tracklist of the album, starting with "Kiss Me", which was revealed at the June 13 concert in Los Angeles, CA. This was followed by reveals of "Petal" on June 14, and "Stay" on June 17, also in in Los Angeles, CA. Ariana launched an additional "opal girly" series of merchandise, lenticular physical versions, and fan pack CD + hoodie bundles of the album on June 18, 2026. Later, the entire tracklist for the album was eventually revealed via the 15-minute countdown to the start of the show on June 19, 2026, in Inglewood, CA, due to leaks. The official Team Ariana account on Instagram also went live for the length of the countdown.


On June 22, 2026, the album was uploaded to streaming services, with six songs marked as explicit: "Petal", "Big Feelings", "Freak", "Like I Do", "Never Get Over Me", and "Bad Thing (Bunny Hop)". On July 8, 2026, Ariana posted snippets and lyrics of "Oh Well" and “Freak” on Instagram, along with releasing a webstore exclusive picture disk variant of the album. Later during the Eternal Sunshine Tour night two in Atlanta, Georgia, Grandes Team went live on instagram at exactly 8:08 PM EST. The title track of the album was teased with attached vocals and revealed to be the instrumental during the opening interlude of the tour. Grande later confirmed it was in fact the title track via Instagram.


On July 16, 2026, Grande posted a photo dump on Instagram confirming the title track as the album's second single. Later on July 18, 2026, during the night four show of The Eternal Sunshine Tour in Brooklyn, a snippet of the album's opening track, "Kiss Me", and a trailer for the "petal" music video were played. On July 24, 2026, lengths of each track were finally revealed via streaming services. Billboards of the album's lyrics appeared across the world on July 26. On July 29, 2026, Ariana performed the title track for the first time ever in Montreal, and released a webstore-exclusive "Process Is Fun" digital download version of the album with seven bonus voice note recordings of the album's creation. Leading up to the release of the album on July 30, 2026, Grande appeared on "Petal Radio" exclusively on Apple Music to answer questions from fans. She also teased the "Petal" music video with a short clip.


Following the release of the album on July 31, 2026, Grande partnered with Uber Eats to promote her album in select cities, where you could order a bouquet and receive an additional Petal vinyl. It was also promoted on TikTok with a profile decoration campaign. The music video for the title track was released later that day at 8AM PST.


Commercial Performance & Critical Reception ⋆ . ˚


Petal debuted at number one on the ARIA Albums Chart (Australia), Official Aotearoa Music Charts (New Zealand) and the UK Albums Chart, marking her sixth chart-topper in those nations. It also topped Australia's Vinyl Chart.


The album debuted at number 1 on the US Billboard 200 with 295,000 album-equivalent units being Grande's seventh number-one album in the United States. This marks her biggest week by units in the 2020s and by pure album sales in more than a decade. Also, she ties for the fifth-most leaders among solo women on the chart, with Taylor Swift (15), followed by Barbra Streisand (11), Madonna (10), Beyoncé (eight) and Janet Jackson, Lady Gaga and Grande (seven each).


The thing that most of the critics have agreed on is that this is Grande's most experimental album yet, full of sounds and beats she has never sang to before. The album was met with mostly positive criticism. Many critics has described the album as an "unfiltered anger against the tabloid mill and fake fans online". For example, Rolling Stone has stated that "the rage suits her musically", calling her "f--- it attitude" loud, clear, and Grande at her absolute best. The album was also described as an "old-fashioned triumph". On the other hand, some negative reviews said that the album is way too experimental for Grande, or found her lyricism to be way too plain and uninteresting.


Apparently, the most notable tracks from the album are "Petal" - describing it as the album's centerpiece, its lead single "Hate That I Made You Love Me", the opening track called "Kiss Me" for its etheral pop production, or the track "Bad Thing (Bunny Hop)" due to it allegedly being the most experimental one. Grande was praised for not being scared to release experimental tracks full of unfiltered lyrics and stepping out of her comfort zone, while using new sounds that are uncomparable to her previous R&B sound.


Tour & Deluxe Details ⋆ . ˚


The album was promoted and performed live during The Eternal Sunshine Tour, with the album's lead single: "Hate That I Made You Love Me", performed at each date. Later, starting with the shows in Montreal, Canada, Grande has performed "Petal" on July 28, "Like I Do" on July 30, "Stay" and "Oh Well" on July 31, and "Big Feelings" and "Never Get Over Me" on August 3.


On July 16, 2026, Grande posted a blurred version of the album's tracklist on Instagram, of which around three bonus tracks can be made out at the bottom. It was also rumored that some alternative physical vinyl variants will include these bonus tracks, which was then denied. It is now rumored to be a slightly deluxe version with remixes of the songs being featured by various different artists.
